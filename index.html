Doughnut Economy by Country live data

Visualize the doughnut of each country, and compare social-foundation and ecological-ceiling performance relative to their boundaries. An ideal society falls within the green doughnut. Red shows the magnitude of shortfall (inside) or overshoot (outside). Hover or tap a bar for details.

Clone of doughnut-economy-fxs7576.netlify.app (static, last updated 2022-08-27, data ~2018–2021) — rebuilt here with live World Bank data for the social foundation.
